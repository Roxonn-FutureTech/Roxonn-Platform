# Withdrawal Process Tutorial for ROXN Token Bounties

This guide explains how developers and pool managers can claim and withdraw their earned ROXN tokens from the Roxonn Platform.

## Prerequisites
Before claiming, ensure you have the following:
- A Web3 Wallet (MetaMask is recommended).
- The wallet must be configured to the **XDC Network** (XinFin).
- A small amount of **XDC tokens** in your wallet to cover potential network fees (gas is paid in XDC, not in ROXN).

## Step-by-Step Guide to Claim Bounties

1. **Connect Your Wallet**
   Go to [app.roxonn.com](https://app.roxonn.com) and connect your wallet using the "Connect Wallet" button in the top right corner.

2. **Navigate to Your Dashboard**
   Once connected, go to your Dashboard or Profile section where your earned bounties are displayed.

3. **Locate the Claim Button**
   Find the specific bounty you want to claim and click the **"Claim"** button. 

4. **Automated Relayer Process**
   Unlike traditional manual withdrawals, Roxonn uses a relayer system. When you click "Claim", the platform's backend automatically processes the transaction and sends the ROXN tokens to your connected wallet. You do not need to manually sign a withdrawal transaction in MetaMask for this step.

5. **Wait for Confirmation**
   The tokens will be transferred to your wallet once the relayer confirms the transaction on the XDC network (usually takes a few seconds).

## How to View Your ROXN Tokens
If you don't see your ROXN tokens in MetaMask after claiming, you may need to import the token manually:
1. Open MetaMask and ensure you are on the XDC Network.
2. Click on "Import Tokens".
3. Paste the official ROXN token contract address (available in the Roxonn platform or documentation).
4. The token will appear in your wallet balance.

## Troubleshooting
- **Transaction Failed**: Ensure you have enough XDC for gas fees.
- **Tokens not showing**: Double-check that you have imported the official ROXN token contract address into your MetaMask wallet.
