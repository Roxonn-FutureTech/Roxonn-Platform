# Withdrawal Process Tutorial for ROXN Token Bounties

This guide explains how developers and pool managers can withdraw their earned ROXN tokens from the Roxonn Platform to their personal wallets.

## Prerequisites
Before withdrawing, ensure you have the following:
- A Web3 Wallet (MetaMask is recommended).
- The wallet must be configured to the **XDC Network** (XinFin).
- A small amount of **XDC tokens** in your wallet to pay for gas fees (transaction fees on the XDC network).

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Connect Your Wallet**
   Go to [app.roxonn.com](https://app.roxonn.com) and connect your wallet using the "Connect Wallet" button in the top right corner.

2. **Navigate to Dashboard**
   Once connected, go to your Dashboard or Profile section where your earned bounties are displayed.

3. **Locate the Withdrawal Section**
   Find the "Withdraw" or "Claim Bounty" section. You will see the total balance of ROXN tokens available for withdrawal.

4. **Enter Amount**
   Input the amount of ROXN tokens you wish to withdraw. Ensure you leave enough for gas fees.

5. **Confirm Transaction**
   Click "Withdraw". A MetaMask popup will appear asking you to confirm the transaction. Review the gas fee and confirm.

6. **Wait for Confirmation**
   The tokens will be transferred to your wallet once the transaction is confirmed on the XDC network (usually takes a few seconds).

## Troubleshooting
- **Transaction Failed**: Ensure you have enough XDC for gas fees.
- **Tokens not showing**: Make sure you have imported the ROXN token contract address into your MetaMask wallet to view your balance.
